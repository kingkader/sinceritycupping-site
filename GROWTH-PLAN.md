## Part 1 — Eliminate £4,099/month of agency fees (Days 1–30)

### 1a. Replace the £500/month SEO retainer with the automated playbook you already own

The sinceritycupping.co.uk repo is a free, self-running SEO machine: static HTML on free hosting, 15 programmatic local-area pages, LocalBusiness/FAQ schema, `llms.txt` for AI search engines, and a GitHub Action that publishes two SEO articles per week with zero ongoing cost. It already ranks.

**Action:** replicate the same structure for sincerityruqyah.co.uk:

- **Condition pages** (these are the searches people in distress actually make): ruqyah for evil eye (ayn), ruqyah for black magic (sihr), ruqyah for jinn possession, ruqyah for waswas, ruqyah for marriage problems, ruqyah before marriage, ruqyah for children, ruqyah for the home.
- **City pages — UK-wide, not just London.** Your Zoom service has no geography. Create "Online ruqyah for Birmingham / Manchester / Bradford / Leeds / Luton / Leicester / Glasgow / Cardiff…" pages. Every UK city with a large Muslim community is a market for the £20 Sunday session and £65 Zoom sessions, with zero extra delivery cost.
- **Twice-weekly auto-articles** using the same `generate-articles.mjs` pattern, answering questions like "how do I know if I have the evil eye", "what to expect in a ruqyah session", "self-ruqyah at home".

Cancel the £500/month retainer once this is live. **Saving: £6,000/year.**

### 1b. Take Google Business Profile in-house — 15 minutes a week instead of £99/month

The weekly routine the agency charges £99/month for:

1. Reply to every new review (2 min).
2. Post one Google Post — Sunday Zoom reminder, upcoming Sunnah cupping days (17th/19th/21st lunar), or a short tip (5 min).
3. Add 1–2 photos (2 min).
4. Answer any new Q&A and seed one question yourself per month (3 min).
5. Keep services + prices current (occasional).

**Saving: £1,188/year.**

### 1c. Taper Google Ads from £3,500/month to ≤£500/month self-managed

Don't switch ads off overnight — taper as organic replaces them:

- **Month 1:** cancel the management fee. Rebuild as one simple self-managed campaign: exact-match only ("ruqyah london", "ruqyah near me", "ruqyah session", "online ruqyah"), your postcode radius + UK-wide for Zoom terms, £15–20/day cap.
- **Month 2–3:** watch Search Console as the new ruqyah pages index. Each week organic bookings rise, cut the daily budget.
- **Month 4:** keep only a small always-on branded + "ruqyah near me" campaign (~£300–500/month) or kill it entirely.

**Saving: £36,000+/year once tapered.**

> Part 1 alone recovers ~£43,000–49,000/year. If your current net income is in the £40–60k range, this is already most of the doubling — before selling a single extra session.

## Part 2 — Grow revenue (Days 1–90)

### 2a. Scale the Sunday Zoom — your highest-margin product (Days 1–14)

25 clients × £20 = £500/week. The marginal cost of attendee #26 is zero. This is the single most scalable thing you own.

- **Grow Sunday to 50+** — promote it on every channel: GBP posts, WhatsApp broadcast to past clients, a banner on both websites, mention at the end of every 1:1 session ("keep your protection up between sessions — £20 on Sundays").
- **Add a second weekly session** (e.g. Wednesday 8pm) once Sunday passes ~40. A different day captures people who can't do Sundays and an evening slot suits Gulf/US time zones — your Zoom market is global.
- **Push the monthly subscription** (you already list £65/month). Frame at checkout and in the session: "4–5 sessions for the price of 3." 40 subscribers = £2,600/month *recurring* before anyone books a single session.

Target: 2 sessions/week × 50 average = **£8,600/month** vs £2,165 today (**+£6,400/month**).

### 2b. Sell treatment plans, not single sessions (Days 1–30)

Ruqyah cases routinely need multiple sessions; right now you re-sell every session one at a time.

- **Course of 4 ruqyah sessions: £240** (vs £260) — paid upfront.
- **Intensive course of 8: £460** (vs £520).
- **Ruqyah + Hijama combination visit: £110** (vs £130 separately) — hijama is the natural Sunnah companion to ruqyah, and you already deliver both under one roof. Offering the combo to every ruqyah client is the fastest way to lift cupping above 15–20%.
- **Aftercare bundle:** ruqyah course + 1 counselling session — lifts counselling above 3% without selling it separately.

Packages raise average transaction value ~30–60%, improve attendance, and smooth cashflow. Set them up as Fresha memberships/packages — no new tooling.

### 2c. Correct your pricing (Day 1)

You are the most-reviewed centre in your market (1,200+ five-star reviews, 13+ years) and you charge *less* than smaller competitors — Riaz Healing Centre charges £70 in person, Fitrah Centre charges £45–70 with phone sessions at £70.

- Individual session £65 → **£75** (face-to-face), keep Zoom at £65.
- Couples/group £85 → **£95**.
- **Home visits: £120 minimum** + travel. At 5% of bookings with travel time, £85 underprices your day badly.
- Keep the Sunday Zoom at £20 — it's your funnel, not your profit centre.

A ~12% rise on 1:1 work flows straight to the bottom line. Existing package holders keep old rates for 60 days; nobody with 1,200 five-star reviews loses clients over £10.

### 2d. Build the rebooking engine (Days 14–45)

The cheapest client is the one you already treated:

- **WhatsApp broadcast list** (you already run the number): Sunday-session reminder every Friday, Sunnah hijama days (17th/19th/21st lunar) monthly, one beneficial reminder per week. WhatsApp Business broadcast is free.
- **90-day hijama recall:** quarterly cupping is the standard cadence — message every past cupping client at day 80.
- **Referral offer:** "Refer a brother or sister — you both get £10 off." Costs nothing until it works.
- **Gift vouchers** on Fresha — Ramadan, Eid, weddings.

### 2e. Productise your knowledge — income that doesn't need your hours (Days 30–90)

Your 1:1 income is capped by hours in the day; these aren't:

- **Self-Ruqyah home programme** — recorded course (videos + PDF + recitation audio) at **£49–79**. Competitors sell these from $25 to $59+ and entire businesses (alruqya.com, ruqyah.org) run on this model. Record it once over a weekend; sell it on the site, to the Zoom audience, and as aftercare for every 1:1 client. 20 sales/month = £1,200–1,500/month passive.
- **Raqi training course** — 13+ years of experience is a certifiable curriculum. Competitors (International Academy of Ruqyah, D2D Therapies, AMAU Academy) charge hundreds per student. One weekend cohort per quarter, 10 students × £397 = **£3,970/quarter (~£1,300/month)**.
- **YouTube ruqyah recitations** — you already have the "Ruqyah Collection" page. Publishing full-length recitations costs nothing, builds authority, and every video description funnels to the £20 Sunday session and the self-ruqyah course. This becomes your free, compounding replacement for Google Ads.
- **Clinic retail:** sidr leaves, black seed oil, olive oil, ruqyah water-prep kits at the desk and as add-ons at online checkout. Small per-ticket, ~50%+ margin, zero extra time.

### 2f. Cross-sell traffic between your two sites — **already implemented in this repo**

Changes shipped with this plan on sinceritycupping.co.uk:

- "Sister centre" section on the homepage promoting ruqyah sessions (£65/£85), the £20 Sunday Zoom, and the hijama + ruqyah combination.
- A ruqyah service row on the Services page with booking links.
- FAQ entry "Do you also offer ruqyah?" on both pages, including in FAQ schema — eligible to surface directly in Google results and AI answers.
- Schema `parentOrganization` + `sameAs` links connecting the two domains, so Google and AI search engines treat sincerityruqyah.co.uk as the same trusted entity.
- `llms.txt` / `llms-full.txt` sister-centre sections so AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) recommend your ruqyah services with prices when asked.
- Footer links on every page template, preserved by the article generator on future runs.
