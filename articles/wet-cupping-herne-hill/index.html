Wet cupping near Herne Hill

This guide explains travel, preparation and what to expect for people researching wet cupping Herne Hill in Herne Hill and nearby South London. Wet cupping is also known as hijama; the clinic uses cupping as its primary term and keeps the practical details clear.

The short answer

Wet cupping / Hijama should come with a clear price, duration, privacy arrangement and explanation of the process. Sincerity Cupping Clinic publishes separate choices for women and men before a booking leaves the website.

Before any cups are placed

The practitioner explains the appointment, asks for relevant information and checks consent. Nothing begins until there has been an opportunity to ask questions and say what feels comfortable.

  • Women see Sister Aisha Mejri, the women's cupping practitioner.
  • Men see Brother Abu Layla, the men's cupping practitioner.
  • New single-use cups and blades are prepared for each client.
  • Written clinic information is provided when the appointment ends.

Evidence and safety

Cupping is complementary care, not a replacement for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. The National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health guide to cupping explains that research is limited and describes possible side effects.

Personal medical questions need an appropriately qualified healthcare professional. The clinic can explain its service and operational policy, but it does not diagnose conditions or direct changes to prescribed care.
