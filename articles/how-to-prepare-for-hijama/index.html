How to prepare for hijama: food, water and what to wear

A little preparation makes a hijama appointment easier. Here is how to prepare, as our Streatham clinic asks clients to do before they arrive.

Wet cupping from £45. Women and men. Open daily.

Book now

Stop eating six hours before your appointment

Do not eat for at least six hours before your hijama session. Keep drinking water, complete the patient form when you book, and wear loose clothing.

Food and drink before hijama

  • Do not eat for at least six hours before your session. A morning appointment means most of those hours pass overnight.
  • Drink plenty of water in the hours and days before you come.
  • Do not smoke or drink alcohol before your session, or after it.

Tell your practitioner before the day

Complete the patient form when you book. Use it to share any medical conditions, medicines or allergies, so your practitioner knows before you arrive. If you are not sure hijama is right for you, read who should check with a doctor first.

What to wear

Wear comfortable, loose clothing. The areas being cupped need to be uncovered, so avoid tight layers and complicated fastenings. Our guide on what to wear to a hijama appointment has more detail.

For Muslim clients: intention and wudu

If you are Muslim, make a sincere intention (niyyah) to seek healing from Allah, and make wudu before your session. Everyone else is just as welcome. You do not need a religious reason to book.

On the day

Your appointment starts with a private consultation, so there is time to ask questions before anything begins. A standard session takes 40 to 45 minutes. See what happens during a wet cupping session, and plan ahead with our aftercare guide.
