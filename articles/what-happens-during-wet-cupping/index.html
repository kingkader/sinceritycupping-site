What happens during a wet cupping (hijama) session?

If you have never had hijama, it helps to know each step before you arrive. Nothing begins until your practitioner has explained it and you are happy to go ahead.

Wet cupping from £45. Women and men. Open daily.

A wet cupping session has four stages

A wet cupping session starts with a private consultation. Then the cups are placed, small surface openings are made and the cups are reapplied, and the area is cleaned and dressed.

1. Private consultation

You talk with your practitioner in private. You agree which points will be cupped, and you can ask anything you like. A standard appointment covers up to ten cups, and can include the sunnah points if you want them.

2. Cup placement

You lie down on a treatment bed. Your practitioner places single-use cups on the skin and creates suction with a hand pump. We do not use fire or mouth suction.

3. Wet cupping

After a few minutes the cups are removed. Small, superficial openings are made in the skin with a single-use blade, and the cups are briefly reapplied to draw out a small amount of blood.

4. Cleaning and dressing

The cups are removed, the area is cleaned and dressed, and you are given aftercare information to take home. Our aftercare guide explains the first few days.

How long does a session take?

Women’s wet cupping is a 45-minute appointment and men’s wet cupping is 40 minutes. Both cost £45. Longer appointments are listed on the Cupping & prices page.

Who will I see, and what is used?

  • Women see Sister Aisha Mejri. Men see Brother Abu Layla.
  • Cups and blades are single-use: used once for one client, then disposed of safely.
  • Bed covers are single-use and your practitioner wears gloves.
  • If anything feels uncomfortable at any point, say so straight away.

Before you come

Do not eat for six hours before your session. The full list is in how to prepare for hijama.
