Male hijama therapist in South London: privacy and booking

Men looking for a male hijama therapist in South London can use the men’s route with Brother Abu Layla at the Streatham clinic, then check the live Fresha service label, price and duration before confirming.

The male practitioner for men is Brother Abu Layla

Brother Abu Layla is the published men’s cupping practitioner for private men’s appointments at Sincerity Cupping Clinic in Streatham. The clinic’s published practitioner profile says he explains each stage before beginning and follows the clinic’s hygiene process throughout.

Check the men’s Fresha labels before confirming

The website’s booking buttons open Fresha under the Sincerity Ruqyah Centre name at the same 330 Streatham High Road address. The £45 men’s Fresha route selected “General Cupping (upto 10 cups) (Male)” for 40 minutes when this guide was checked on 7 October 2026; the £80 men’s Fresha route selected “Full Body Cupping (Male)” for 1 hr 20 min.

Use those labels as a booking check, not as medical or religious advice. Fresha is the confirmation screen, so check the service, date, time, price and any route note shown there before you confirm. If any wording on Fresha conflicts with the men’s service you intended to book, stop and contact the clinic first.

The two men’s choices are different services

  • Men’s wet cupping: £45 and 40 minutes on the website, selected on Fresha as “General Cupping (upto 10 cups) (Male)”.
  • Men’s longer full-body/spiritual appointment: £80 and 80 minutes on the website, selected on Fresha as “Full Body Cupping (Male)”.

Do not choose the longer appointment just because it costs more. If you are unsure which practical service matches what you want to book, ask the clinic to explain the difference before choosing a time.

Cupping is complementary care, not a replacement for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. The National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health guide to cupping explains that research is limited and describes possible side effects.

Personal medical questions need an appropriately qualified healthcare professional. The clinic can explain its service and operational policy, but it does not diagnose conditions or direct changes to prescribed care.
