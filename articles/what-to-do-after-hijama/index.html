What to do after hijama: aftercare for the first days

Good aftercare is simple. This is the advice our Streatham clinic gives clients for the hours and days after a hijama session.

Rest, drink water and keep the cupped areas dry

After hijama, rest, drink plenty of water and eat light food. Keep the cupped areas dry for the first 6 to 12 hours, and leave hard exercise for two to three days.

The first 24 hours

  • Rest. Give your body time to recover.
  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Eat light food. Honey and dates are a good choice. Leave heavy meals for later.
  • Keep the cupped areas dry for the first 6 to 12 hours. You can shower, but keep the cupped areas out of the water at first.
  • Do not scratch or pick at the cupped areas. They heal on their own.
  • Do not smoke.
  • Some practitioners advise leaving cold food and cold drinks for 24 hours.
  • Avoid sexual intercourse for 24 hours.

The next few days

  • Keep the areas clean. Wash gently with water, then apply a little olive oil or black seed oil.
  • Leave hard exercise and heavy physical work for two to three days.
  • Keep drinking water.

What is normal after hijama?

Round marks, a little like bruises, appear where the cups were. They usually fade within 3 to 10 days. Mild soreness where the cups were is also normal, and usually settles within a day or two.

When to get in touch
