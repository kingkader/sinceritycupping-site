About

Trusted wet cupping care in South West London

Sincerity Cupping Clinic is a specialised hijama clinic offering insured wet cupping for men and women in a clean, serene setting.

Our approach

Listen first, treat with care

Located in Streatham, Sincerity Cupping Clinic focuses on one service done properly: traditional wet cupping, also known as hijama. The service is available for both male and female clients and is led by expert practitioners with 40 years of combined experience.

The clinic combines traditional practice, modern client care, full insurance and a calm environment for people who want to feel prepared before treatment starts.

  • Wet cupping sessions built around your goals and suitability.
  • Clear practitioner communication before and after treatment.
  • Clean, serene and insured care in Streatham, South London.
