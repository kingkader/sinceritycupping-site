Private cupping · Streatham

A cupping clinic built around privacy and clear care

Sincerity Cupping Clinic welcomes women and men of every faith and background. We explain the process plainly, respect your boundaries and keep medical decisions with qualified healthcare professionals.

Everyone is welcome

Cupping without assumptions

Some clients know wet cupping by the Arabic name hijama. Others simply call it cupping. You do not need a particular faith, ethnicity or reason to ask about an appointment.

Our setup is deliberately straightforward: women see a women's cupping practitioner and men see a men's cupping practitioner. Privacy, consent and dignity guide every visit.

How we work

Four simple commitments

  1. 01

    Explain first

    You can ask questions and understand the process before anything begins.

  2. 02

    Protect privacy

    Women's and men's appointments are kept clearly separate.

  3. 03

    Use single-use equipment

    New cups and blades are prepared for each client and disposed of after use.

  4. 04

    Keep boundaries clear

    Cupping is complementary care and does not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
