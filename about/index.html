About the clinic

One service, done properly, for twenty years and counting

Sincerity Cupping Clinic does one thing: traditional wet cupping, delivered to a clinical standard for men and women in Streatham. No upsells, no exaggerated promises — just careful hijama.

Our approach

Listen first, treat with care

Most clients find us one of two ways: a recommendation from someone we've treated, or a search for hijama done safely nearby. Either way, the first thing we do is listen — your health history, your goals, your privacy preferences — and the last thing we do is hand you written aftercare.

We combine traditional practice with modern client care: full insurance, single-use sterile equipment, honest suitability checks and a calm, private room on Streatham High Road.

  • Sessions built around your suitability, never a fixed script.
  • Clear communication before, during and after treatment.
  • A respectful welcome whether it's your first session or your fortieth.

What guides us

Three commitments behind every appointment

Integrity

You'll always hear what cupping is, what it is not, and when seeing a doctor matters more. We refuse bookings we shouldn't take.

Professionalism

Prepared rooms, opened-in-front-of-you equipment, punctual appointments and records of what was agreed — from booking to aftercare.

Compassion

Every session starts with the person in front of us — nervous first-timers get more time, not less patience.
