A direct answer

What is wet cupping?

Wet cupping is a traditional complementary practice. Cups create suction on the skin, then small superficial openings are made with a single-use blade before the cups are briefly reapplied. We explain each stage before starting and provide written aftercare when the appointment ends.

A direct answer

Is the clinic only for Muslims?

No. The clinic welcomes women and men from every faith, ethnicity and background. Some people know the practice by its Arabic name, while others simply call it wet cupping. You do not need a religious reason to book, and our same-sex setup is there for privacy and comfort.

A direct answer

How do I prepare for hijama?

Do not eat for at least six hours before your session. Keep drinking water, wear loose clothing and complete the patient form when you book. Afterwards, rest, keep the cupped areas dry for the first 6 to 12 hours and leave hard exercise for two to three days.

A direct answer

When are the sunnah days for hijama?

The sunnah days are the 17th, 19th and 21st of each Islamic month. Hijama is still allowed on any other day when you need it. Our guide lists the next sunnah days as UK calendar dates.

Four calm, clearly explained steps

Nothing begins until you know what to expect and have had the chance to ask questions.

  1. 01

    Private consultation

  2. 02

    Cup placement
