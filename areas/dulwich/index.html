Frequently asked by Dulwich clients

Is there parking near the clinic?

There is on-street parking on Streatham High Road and nearby side streets. Restrictions vary by time of day, so please check signage. Many clients also arrive by bus or train.

How long is a session?

A standard session takes 60 to 75 minutes including consultation, cupping and aftercare. First visits can run slightly longer.

Can I book a same-sex practitioner?

Yes. Please mention your preference at booking — female clients can request Sister Aisha, and male clients can request Brother Abu Layla.
