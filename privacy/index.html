Privacy notice

This page explains what happens when you contact the clinic or follow an external booking, map or messaging link.

Last updated: 21 July 2026. The controller is AXONIS LTD trading as Sincerity Cupping Clinic, 330 Streatham High Rd, London SW16 6HH.

Personal data we may handle

Contact and booking information can include your name, telephone number, email address, appointment selection, booking details and messages you send. Safety and consultation information can include information you choose to provide so the clinic can discuss whether and how it can provide the booked service. This may include health information. Technical logs may include an IP address, browser information, requested page, date and time when the website host records a request.

The contact form prepares an email in your own email application. The website does not submit that form to its own database. Your email provider and our email provider handle the resulting message.

Why we use data and our lawful bases

  • To answer an enquiry, arrange a booking and take steps at your request before entering a contract, or to perform a contract for a booked service.
  • To administer appointments, protect the website and clinic systems, respond to service questions and keep proportionate business records where this is necessary for our legitimate interests and those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • To keep records, respond to regulators or authorities, and meet tax, accounting or other requirements where processing is necessary for a legal obligation.
  • Where you have a real choice, for a stated purpose based on consent. You may withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal does not make earlier processing unlawful.

Special category data

Health information is special category data. Where the clinic asks for and uses safety and consultation information with your explicit consent, the Article 6 basis is consent or contract steps as applicable and the Article 9 condition is explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a). We may instead need Article 9(2)(f) where processing is necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. If no Article 9 condition applies, we will not use special category data for that purpose. Do not send detailed health information through a general website enquiry.

Booking, hosting and communications providers

Booking links open Fresha, which handles booking data under its own privacy information and our arrangements with it. Website hosting providers may process technical logs. Email, telephone and WhatsApp providers carry communications. Google Maps handles map and direction requests. Depending on the service and contract, a provider may act as our processor or as a separate controller.

We may also disclose relevant data to professional advisers, insurers, regulators, law-enforcement bodies or courts where needed for advice, a legal claim or a legal obligation. We assess disclosure against the purpose and information requested.

Retention

The retention criteria are why the information was collected, whether an enquiry or appointment remains active, applicable accounting or legal requirements, possible disputes and the need to demonstrate what was agreed. This notice does not state a fixed period where one has not been verified. You can ask which criteria and current period apply to a particular record.

Security

This website is static and its contact form does not create a website database. Booking and message data instead sit in the provider services described above, so security also depends on those providers, the clinic's service accounts and the devices used to access them. No website, device or communications service can be made completely secure.

International transfers

Some hosting, booking, email, telephone, WhatsApp, map or support providers may process data outside the UK. Their current privacy information describes locations and transfer arrangements used within their services. UK data protection law requires a valid mechanism for a restricted international transfer, which may be an adequacy regulation, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or another lawful safeguard. Contact us for the provider and mechanism identified for your data; this notice does not claim that one mechanism applies to every provider.

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and the right to object. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw consent. A right may be limited by an exemption or by the lawful basis being used. We may need enough information to confirm your identity and understand the request.

Questions and complaints

Contact AXONIS LTD trading as Sincerity Cupping Clinic at sincerityruqyah@gmail.com, call 07552 540000, write to 330 Streatham High Rd, London SW16 6HH, or use the contact page.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. See the ICO's guide to making a data protection complaint.
