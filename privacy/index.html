Privacy notice

This page explains what happens when you contact the clinic or follow an external booking, map or messaging link.

Last updated: 21 July 2026. The controller is AXONIS LTD trading as Sincerity Cupping Clinic, 330 Streatham High Rd, London SW16 6HH.

Personal data we may handle

Contact and booking information can include your name, telephone number, email address, chosen service, appointment time, booking details and messages. Safety and consultation information is optional health information you expressly supply during an appointment safety conversation. Financial and legal records can include payment, invoice, complaint and claims information. Technical logs can include an IP address, browser information, requested page, date and time.

The contact form prepares an email through a mailto link in your own email application. It does not submit the form to a database on this website. If you send the prepared message, your email provider and the clinic's Google/Gmail account handle it.

What is needed and what is optional

Contact details, chosen service and appointment time are needed to arrange and administer an appointment. The general enquiry form also needs the marked contact fields and a message so your email application can prepare a usable enquiry.

Consultation health information is optional: you decide whether to provide it and whether to give explicit consent to its use for appointment safety. Detailed health information must not be sent through the general enquiry. Declining necessary in-person safety questions may mean the clinic cannot proceed with the appointment safely.

Special category data and legal claims

Health information is special category data. We use Article 9(2)(a) when you give explicit consent for appointment-safety processing. You may withdraw that consent at any time, without making earlier processing unlawful. We rely on Article 9(2)(f) only when information is needed for an actual legal claim that must be established, exercised or defended; it is not the clinic's general basis for consultation information. If no Article 9 condition applies, we do not use special category data for that purpose.

Providers and external links

  • Fresha provides booking and appointment administration. Read Fresha's privacy policy.
  • Google/Gmail provides the clinic email account. Google Maps is an external directions service; Google Maps receives technical information only when you choose a directions link. Read Google's privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp/Meta carries messages you choose to send. Read the WhatsApp privacy policy for the UK and EEA.
  • Cloudflare provides website delivery and security services and may handle the technical logs described above. Read Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Depending on the service and contract, a provider may act as our processor or as a separate controller. We may also disclose relevant data to professional advisers, insurers, regulators, law-enforcement bodies or courts where needed for advice, an actual legal claim or a legal obligation.

Retention

We keep each category only until the relevant end criterion is met, subject to deletion holds required for an actual dispute, legal claim or legal obligation:

  • Email and WhatsApp enquiries: until the enquiry is resolved and any agreed follow-up has ended.
  • Fresha booking records: until the booking administration, complaint and payment period has ended.
  • Consultation safety information: until the consultation relationship and any insurer or legal-claims period has ended.
  • Financial records: until the statutory financial-record period has ended.
  • Cloudflare logs: until Cloudflare's security-log period has ended.

Provider backup or deletion cycles can finish later under the provider's own documented schedule. You can ask which criterion currently applies to a particular record.

Security

This website is static and its contact form does not create a website database. Booking and message data instead sit in the provider services described above, so security also depends on the website hosting environment, those providers, the clinic's service accounts and the devices used to access them. No website, device or communications service can be made completely secure.

International transfers

Fresha, Google/Gmail, WhatsApp/Meta and Cloudflare may process information outside the UK as described in their linked privacy information. The applicable mechanism must be identified for the provider and processing location involved. Depending on the destination, that may mean provider-specific adequacy safeguards, a UK Addendum or an IDTA. Contact us if you want the mechanism identified for a particular provider; this notice does not claim that one mechanism applies to every service.

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and the right to object. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw consent. A right may be limited by an exemption or by the lawful basis being used. We may need enough information to confirm your identity and understand the request.
