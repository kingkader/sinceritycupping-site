Plain-English policy

Privacy notice

This page explains what happens when you contact the clinic or follow an external booking, map or messaging link.

Last updated: 20 July 2026. Sincerity Cupping Clinic operates from 330 Streatham High Rd, London SW16 6HH.

Information you choose to send

If you call, use WhatsApp or email the clinic, we receive the information you choose to provide so we can reply and manage your enquiry. Please do not send detailed medical information through the website enquiry form.

The enquiry form

The contact form prepares an email in your own email application. The website does not submit the form to a database or store a copy. Your email provider handles the message under its own privacy terms.

Booking, maps and messaging

Booking links take you to Fresha. Direction links and the map use Google Maps. WhatsApp links open WhatsApp. When you use those services, their privacy terms apply and they may process technical or account information independently of this website.

Website technology

This is a static website. Its layout and fonts are delivered without a third-party font service. Essential hosting logs may include an IP address, browser information, the requested page and time of access for security and reliability.

How clinic messages are used

Messages are used to answer enquiries, organise appointments and maintain necessary business records. Access is limited to people who need the information for those purposes. We do not sell enquiry details.

Your choices

You can ask what personal information the clinic holds about you, request a correction, or ask a privacy question. Some information may need to be retained where the law requires it.
