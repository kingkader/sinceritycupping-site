Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Six of the most common — the full list of 42 lives on the FAQ page.

What is ruqyah?

Islamic spiritual treatment through recitation of the Qur'an and authentic supplications — rooted in the Qur'an and Sunnah, never superstition or illegitimate practices.

What happens during a session?

A first session typically includes consultation, spiritual diagnosis and intensive ruqyah recitation, followed by clear aftercare guidance. Reactions vary — your raqi explains what's normal.

How should I prepare?

Make wudu if possible, come with sincere intention, and be ready to focus on the recitation. For hijama, avoid eating for at least six hours before your appointment.

Can ruqyah be done online?

Yes — privately or through weekly group Zoom sessions every Sunday at 6pm UK time (£20, or £65 monthly), open worldwide with cameras off if you prefer.

Do you offer male and female hijama therapists?

Yes, both — book whichever you're comfortable with. Hijama starts from £45 with single-use sterile equipment throughout.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed appointments, forfeit the upfront payment — so if plans change, tell us early and we'll rearrange.
