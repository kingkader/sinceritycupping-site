# Surface brief: homepage (index.html) and shared site chrome

Mode: Persuade. Audience: anyone in South London who wants wet cupping, on a phone, comparing clinics. Action: book on Fresha (women / men) or ask on WhatsApp. Proof: practitioners' years, single-use equipment, opening hours, real reviews. Constraints: PRODUCT.md; 113 site tests; stock photos only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is the clinic's appointment card. Instead of a hero banner over a row of cards, the first screen is a working booking slip: treatment and price, practitioner choice, the week's opening hours, one booking action.

OWN-WORLD: Mineral bone ground (#e7eeec), white card paper, near-black green ink, hairline rules, one clinic-teal accent used only where you act. Schibsted Grotesk for words, Red Hat Mono for every time, price and duration. Cards are slips with rows, not tiles.

STORY: The visitor sees the price and who will treat them, sees the clinic is open today and late, picks women or men, and books. Below: privacy, hygiene, reviews, guides.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Logo top-left, Call and Menu top-right. H1 "Hijama & wet cupping in Streatham." large. One-line lede. The appointment card fills the rest: price row, two practitioner choices side by side, seven-day hours strip with today marked, full-width teal "See times and book" bar visible on a 390x844 phone.

FORM: Appointment card, candidate 3 of my ordered list; seed:910f22d327a29ff1. Raises: from the Rietveld house, colour only on the edge that moves (teal is reserved for actions and the selected choice); from the azulejo hall, whole-unit grid (all spacing on a 4px module, numerals tabular in one mono).

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: choosing a practitioner switches the card: the choice fills teal with a spring, the duration and booking link change to that practitioner's Fresha service.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
