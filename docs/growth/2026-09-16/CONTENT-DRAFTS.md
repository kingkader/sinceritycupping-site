## Draft 1 — replacement section for the existing price guide

Route: `/articles/hijama-cost-london/`

Proposed title: Hijama prices in Streatham: compare the four appointments

Proposed description: Compare Sincerity's £45 wet cupping and £80 spiritual appointments, with separate women's and men's options and clear service durations.

### How much is an appointment?

At Sincerity Cupping Clinic in Streatham, a standard wet cupping appointment costs £45. Women's appointments are listed for 45 minutes; men's appointments are listed for 40 minutes. Longer spiritual appointments cost £80, with 90 minutes listed for women and 80 minutes for men.

These are the clinic's listed choices. They are not an average price for every London clinic. When comparing providers, check the exact service and time included rather than comparing the price alone.

### Which service should I choose?

Start with what you want to book. If you are looking for standard wet cupping, choose the women's or men's £45 appointment. If you are considering a longer spiritual appointment, ask the clinic what that service includes before choosing it. The longer option is a different service; a higher price does not establish a better outcome.

Women book with Sister Aisha Mejri. Men book with Brother Abu Layla. If you have questions about the appointment or your privacy, contact the clinic before selecting a time.

### Why does the booking page say Sincerity Ruqyah Centre?

The cupping clinic is within Sincerity Ruqyah Centre at 330 Streatham High Road, London SW16 6HH. The Fresha booking page uses the centre's name. Before confirming, check the service name, practitioner, time and price shown there.

The website's separate women's and men's booking links take you to the corresponding service. If a suitable time is not shown, call 07552 540000 or message the clinic on WhatsApp. An enquiry does not reserve an appointment; use the booking confirmation to verify your time.

### What should I check before confirming?

Check that you have selected the right appointment, the correct practitioner and a time you can attend. Read any payment or cancellation terms presented during booking. We have not reproduced those terms here because you should check the terms applying to your actual booking.

For current choices, [compare cupping services](/services/). For practical questions, [contact the clinic](/contact/). If this is your first visit, [read the appointment guide](/articles/first-hijama-appointment-streatham/).

Proposed description: Know which appointment to book, where to find the Streatham clinic and what practical questions to ask before your first visit.

### Start with the right appointment

You do not need to know every term before contacting the clinic. Wet cupping is also called hijama. If you are unsure which listed service matches what you are looking for, ask the clinic to explain the choices before booking.

Sincerity offers separate appointments for women and men. Sister Aisha Mejri sees women and Brother Abu Layla sees men. Standard wet cupping is listed at £45, for 45 minutes for women and 40 minutes for men. You can compare these with the longer spiritual appointments on the [services page](/services/).

### Check your confirmation

Bookings open on Fresha under Sincerity Ruqyah Centre. Check the service, practitioner, date and time before confirming. Keep the confirmation so you can check your appointment details again before travelling.

If you cannot find a suitable time, ask the clinic about availability. A WhatsApp message alone is not a confirmed booking. For payment, changes or cancellation, check the terms presented with your booking rather than relying on an old screenshot or article.

### Plan your arrival

The clinic is at 330 Streatham High Road, London SW16 6HH, within Sincerity Ruqyah Centre. Use the [contact page](/contact/) for directions and the clinic's contact details. Check your journey close to your appointment, as transport and traffic can change.

If entrance access, stairs or another practical arrangement matters to your visit, ask the clinic beforehand. We do not assume that a building or treatment space meets your particular access needs without checking.

### Bring your questions

Useful questions include who will see you, how privacy is arranged, what happens during the booked service and who to contact afterwards. Ask about anything you do not understand before agreeing to proceed. You can also ask the clinic about clothing and practical arrangements for your appointment.

Personal medical questions need a qualified healthcare professional. An online booking or general website guide cannot determine whether wet cupping is suitable for you.

### Before you leave

Check that you understand the written information supplied for your appointment and know how to contact the clinic with practical questions. This guide does not replace those instructions or medical advice.
