## Measurement specification — not installed

Separate funnel stages:

- `booking_click`: service identifier, page path, button location and source category. This is an outbound click, not a booking.
- `contact_click`: telephone or WhatsApp, page path and button location. This is intent, not a answered enquiry.
- Confirmed booking, cancellation and attended appointment: aggregate Fresha records using approved account access. Do not collect client names, messages, medical details or guide-search terms into website analytics.

Before instrumentation, inspect the existing analytics account and consent setup; document the actual provider and required privacy changes. Cross-domain attribution depends on Fresha support/access. If unavailable, report clicks and bookings separately with attribution marked unknown. Do not invent conversion rates by joining unrelated totals.

Report new cupping clients, attended appointments, repeat visits and attributable acquisition source where available. Appointment list prices are not net revenue or profit. Keep £0 paid ads. A 28-day baseline precedes numeric growth targets; compare matching days and disclose small samples.
