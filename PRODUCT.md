# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Everyone in South London who wants wet cupping (hijama): people seeking relief, recovery or regular self-care, of every faith and background. Muslim clients who seek hijama on the sunnah days are welcome and served, but the site speaks first to the wider local public. Most visitors arrive on a phone from Google or Maps, compare a few clinics, and want to book in a minute.

## Product Purpose
Sincerity Cupping Clinic offers wet cupping in a private, same-sex setting at 330 Streatham High Road, London SW16 6HH. The website's job is to make a first-time visitor feel calm and confident, then book on Fresha or ask on WhatsApp. Success is more booked appointments from new local clients.

## Positioning
- Experienced, same-sex practitioners: Sister Aisha Mejri (20 years) sees women, Brother Abu Layla (10 years) sees men; Leah (15 years) and Fatuma (5 years) are cupping practitioners.
- Strict hygiene: single-use cups and blades, one client then disposed of.
- Easy to say yes: wet cupping from £45, open 7 days, late (Mon–Fri 10:00–19:00, Sat–Sun 10:00–20:30).

## Operating Context
- Booking happens on Fresha, where the clinic is listed as Sincerity Ruqyah Centre; WhatsApp 07552 540000.
- About 95% of clients today are referred by the Sincerity Ruqyah Centre's practitioners; the site must win new clients from search.
- Clinic advice: no food for 6 hours before; no hard exercise for 2 to 3 days after.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML site (GitHub kingkader/sinceritycupping-site, Cloudflare Pages). `npm test` (113 tests), `npm run build`, `npm run check:dist` must pass; tests pin facts and banned content. Never weaken a test to get content through.
- Services and prices: wet cupping £45 (women 45 min, men 40 min); full body cupping £80; four services on the prices page.
- 29 published guides, area pages (hidden from Google on purpose), sunnah-days guide.
- Must keep: page speed (Lighthouse 100 on the preview), schema, llms.txt, all SEO set-up.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Sincerity Cupping Clinic. Logo: assets/img/sincerity-cupping-logo.svg.
- Voice: plain, calm, honest. No invented claims.
- Must not feel too religious for non-Muslims. Must not look cheap or template-made.
- Visitors should feel "calm, premium self-care" in the first five seconds.

## Evidence on Hand
- Real stock photographs (not AI): back-cupping-glass, cupping-cups-box, glass-cups-drawer (Pexels, cottonbro), wet-cupping-therapy, in assets/img.
- No photos of the real clinic or practitioners yet; do not fabricate them.
- 100+ public reviews across Fresha and Google; real client quotes already on the site. Do not invent reviews or ratings.

## Product Principles
1. Calm first: the visitor should feel looked after before they are sold to.
2. Booking is never more than one tap away.
3. Proof over adjectives: practitioners' years, hygiene facts, real reviews.
4. Welcoming to everyone; faith is respected, not foregrounded.
